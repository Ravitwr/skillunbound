# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not harass, demean, threaten, or exclude people based on identity, background, experience, disability, or access needs. Give feedback about the work, protect learner privacy, attribute sources, and never submit credentials or personal learner data.

Report conduct or security concerns privately to the repository owner until a dedicated support channel is published. Maintainers may remove abusive contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.
